How the tool checks you
  • It checks the displayed numerator and denominator of each factor.
  • Equivalent factors are accepted, including a factor selected from the opposite-direction card and then flipped.
  • Factors may be placed in a different order when the complete setup remains mathematically equivalent.
  • It checks orientation so units cancel correctly.

Step 3: Multiply the numbers (then divide)

Once your setup is correct, multiply all numerator numbers together, multiply all denominator numbers together, then divide.

Example method:
