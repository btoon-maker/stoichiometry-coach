Text version of the Mole Map

Begin with the unit you have. Convert to moles when necessary. Use a mole ratio only when moving from one substance to another. Then convert from moles to the requested unit.

  • Grams to moles: divide by the substance’s molar mass in grams per mole.
  • Moles to grams: multiply by the substance’s molar mass in grams per mole.
  • Particles to moles: divide by 6.022 × 1023 particles per mole.
  • Moles to particles: multiply by 6.022 × 1023 particles per mole.
  • Liters of gas at STP to moles: divide by 22.4 liters per mole.
  • Moles to liters of gas at STP: multiply by 22.4 liters per mole.
  • Substance A to Substance B: use the balanced-equation coefficients as the mole-to-mole conversion ratio.

What students should remember

  • Convert to moles if you aren’t already in moles.
  • Mole ratio always comes from the balanced equation coefficients (only needed when switching substances).
  • Convert out to the unit asked for (grams, particles, liters).
